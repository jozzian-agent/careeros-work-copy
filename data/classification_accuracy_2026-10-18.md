Only rows where the feed states a real value are compared; a feed `unknown` is the feed declining to answer, and scoring against it would measure coverage and call it accuracy. **Agreement is not accuracy**: neither side is a hand label, and the rows marked not independent share their reader with the feed, so their agreement is arithmetic.

A disagreement does not say which side is wrong, and on the small comparable sets above it often says the feed is. Reading the ten contract disagreements by hand: a posting titled "Lehrstelle als Zeichner:in" is an apprenticeship and its feed calls it an internship, and a posting titled "Working Student" is working student work and its feed calls it an internship. The language disagreements are the same shape -- four of the five are German texts the feed labels `fr` or `en`, which is `base.language_record` being honest about what it measures: the language the TENANT publishes a feed variant in, not a reading of the text. Precedence still gives the feed the stored value in every one of these cases, which is the owner's rule and not a judgement about who was right.

## What this report does not say

- Nothing here is tuned to the golden set. The keyword tables are the ones `esco_occupations.yaml` already shipped for the extraction prompt, and the one tie-breaker added is generic (how much of a family's own vocabulary the posting carries), not a rule about any row.
- The three incomplete taxonomy dimensions (CH-ISCO-19's 5-digit list, the NOGA divisions, the municipality register's coordinates) are untouched; occupation is classified at job family plus four-digit ISCO, which is what the shipped lists support (CAREER-77 owns the rest).
- Agreement with a feed is not accuracy, and the two are never added together.
