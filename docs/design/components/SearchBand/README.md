The search block at the top of All jobs, built to look for things. A solid `brand` panel, `radius-600`, 24px padding, with `on-brand` text.

- **Search pill:** one white (`surface`) pill, 56px high, holding a keywords field, a hairline divider, a cities-or-regions field, and a primary "Search" button at the right end. The button is brand green on a white pill, so it reads as the one action.
- **Filter chips** below, one wrapping row: Added since, Workload, the active filters, Company, Industry, Saved, "Fewer filters" (the rest), and a text "Reset filters". Only filters the data can answer are offered; jobs.ch's Contract type and Language are left out because they are not stored. Workload works only when the posting states it.
- **Chip states on the band:** unselected is white with a chevron; selected is solid black with white text and an x (see FilterChip).
- Under the band: the result count, "Hide closed postings", and the SegmentedControl for Tiles and List.
- The band is the page's only brand panel, and All jobs also has the heading's primary "Add a job"; they are different kinds of action.
