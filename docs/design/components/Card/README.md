A Gestalt Box used as a surface. White on the `ground`, 1px `border-decorative`, 16px radius, 24px padding, no shadow.

- Layout inside a card: badge, then heading (heading-sm), then paragraph (body-md in `text-neutral`), 12px apart.
- The current or highlighted card takes a 2px `brand` border instead of the grey one.
- Board job cards are smaller: 12px padding, a stage badge, job title at weight 700, company as an underlined link, location with a pin icon, and a `text-subtle` meta line.
- Large panels (hero board, closing banner) use `brand` fill and `radius-600`; text on them is white.
