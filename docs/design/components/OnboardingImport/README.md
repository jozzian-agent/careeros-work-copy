How a new person gets their background into CareerOS. Step 2 of 3 of onboarding; the same flow opens from "Import from a CV" on the account pages.

1. **Choose:** four cards in a 2 by 2 grid: Upload your CV (tagged "Quickest"), Use your LinkedIn profile, Paste text, Add by hand. "Skip for now" below. A line states that files and text are read by an AI model from a third-party provider, with a link.
2. **Upload:** a dashed dropzone ("Drop a file here", "Choose a file" primary) and an open-and-close "Where do I find my LinkedIn PDF?" with three steps (open your profile, More, Save to PDF, upload it). CareerOS cannot log in to LinkedIn, so LinkedIn data arrives as that PDF or LinkedIn's own data export.
3. **Check what we found:** positions, education and certifications as checkbox lists, each group marked AI-extracted. Items the reader is unsure of start unticked. Primary "Add N items"; nothing is saved before it.
Steps 1 and 3 of onboarding (account, finish) are not designed.
